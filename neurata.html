NEURATA PLATFORM END USER AGREEMENT (Effective March 15, 2026)

1. Acceptance

By creating an account you agree to be bound by this Agreement. If using the platform on behalf of a brokerage or strata corporation, you represent that you have authority to do so.

2. Brokerage & Representative Structure

Brokerage entities are created using verified BCFSA Brokerage Licence Numbers and are immutable. Representatives are associated with one brokerage at a time using their brokerage email address.

3. Strata Corporation Entities

Strata Corporations are independent, immutable entities identified by their LTSA plan number, linked to one brokerage at a time. If a Strata Corporation transfers to a new brokerage, the former brokerage and its representatives lose access. All data is retained by the Strata Corporation.

4. Meeting Mode Trial

Each Strata Corporation receives 2 free adjourned Meeting Mode sessions. Draft meetings and historic minutes ingestion are unlimited and do not count toward the trial. Historic minutes uploaded must correspond to the correct Strata Corporation — mismatched plan numbers will be rejected.

5. StrataSphere & Data Processing

Local StrataSphere. Meeting minutes, agendas, decisions, and uploaded documents are stored and processed within your brokerage's private StrataSphere instance. This data is accessible only to authorized representatives of your brokerage and is never shared with other brokerages or third parties.

Global StrataSphere. If you opt into Global StrataSphere, anonymized and de-identified governance data (motions, decisions, and vote outcomes) may be aggregated across the platform to generate cross-portfolio insights. Before any data is contributed to the Global StrataSphere, Neurata applies automated PII stripping to remove all personally identifiable information including owner names, strata lot identifiers linked to individuals, contact details, and any other information that could identify a natural person.

PII & PIPA Compliance. Neurata processes personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA, BC). Personal information collected during account creation (name, email, BCFSA licence number) is used solely for authentication, account management, and regulatory verification. Meeting minutes and documents may contain personal information about strata lot owners — by uploading such content, you confirm that you have the authority to do so under applicable strata legislation and your brokerage's privacy policies.

Data Residency. All data is stored on servers located in Canada (Toronto region). No personal information is transferred outside of Canada.

AI Processing. Neurata uses AI (Anthropic Claude) to generate meeting minutes summaries and parse uploaded documents. Content sent to the AI is subject to Anthropic's data processing terms. Neurata does not permit Anthropic to use your content to train AI models.

6. Data Ownership

All content related to a Strata Corporation is owned by that Strata Corporation. Neurata will not prevent data portability regardless of subscription or brokerage status.

7. Authorization

By creating an account, you confirm that you are a licensed BCFSA representative in good standing, authorized to act on behalf of your brokerage and any strata corporations assigned to you on the platform.

8. Governing Law

Governed by the laws of British Columbia, Canada.

2. Description of Service

Neurata is a strata management platform designed to assist property management professionals in British Columbia, Canada. The Service includes Meeting Mode (tools for running strata council and general meetings, capturing motions, votes, and generating minutes), StrataSphere (an AI-powered governance intelligence layer for accessing past decisions and portfolio-wide insights), and document management, council member management, and related administrative tools. Neurata is not a legal service and does not provide legal advice. Minutes, motions, and decisions generated through the platform should be reviewed by qualified professionals where appropriate.

4. Subscriptions and Billing

Neurata offers paid subscription services billed in Canadian dollars (CA$) subject to applicable taxes including GST: Meeting Mode + Local StrataSphere at CA$49.00/strata corporation/month + CA$1.49/strata lot/month; Meeting Mode + Global StrataSphere at CA$49.00/strata corporation/month + CA$2.49/strata lot/month. All subscriptions are billed monthly on the first day of each month, in advance. When a subscription is activated mid-month, a prorated charge for the remaining days is collected immediately. Payment is collected via pre-authorized debit (ACSS) or credit card through Stripe. GST (5%) is added to all charges for Canadian customers.

All data is stored on servers located in Canada. Neurata complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of British Columbia and PIPEDA. You retain ownership of all data you upload to the platform. Neurata does not sell your data to third parties.

Local StrataSphere. Meeting minutes, agendas, decisions, and uploaded documents are stored and processed within your brokerage's private StrataSphere instance. This data is accessible only to authorized representatives of your brokerage and is never shared with other brokerages or third parties.

Global StrataSphere. If you opt into Global StrataSphere, anonymized and de-identified governance data (motions, decisions, and vote outcomes) may be aggregated across the platform to generate cross-portfolio insights. Neurata applies automated PII stripping to remove all personally identifiable information before any data is contributed to the Global StrataSphere.

Data Residency. Primary data storage is on servers located in Canada (Toronto region) via Supabase. Neurata uses Cloudflare Workers for API processing (edge network, global) and Anthropic's Claude AI for StrataSphere and minutes generation. AI queries are transmitted to Anthropic's servers for processing. Neurata does not permit Anthropic to use your content to train AI models. Payment processing is handled by Stripe. Transactional email is delivered via MailTrap. Where data is processed outside Canada, Neurata relies on industry-standard data processing agreements with those providers.

Access Requests & Privacy Rights. Under PIPA, individuals have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of their personal information. Contact privacy@neurata.ai. Neurata will respond within 30 days. You may file a complaint with the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC.

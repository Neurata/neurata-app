Security & Governance

Enterprise-grade. Built for regulated environments.

Strata governance involves sensitive owner data, legal records, and regulatory obligations. Neurata is built with the operational maturity that serious organizations require.

Data Sovereignty

All data stored exclusively in Canada. No cross-border transfers. Your governance records never leave Canadian infrastructure.

PIPA Compliant

Personal Information Protection Act compliance built into every layer. PII stripped from cross-portfolio intelligence. Owner data never shared between brokerages.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permissioning across Managing Brokers, Associate Brokers, Strata Managers, and Council Delegates. Every role sees exactly what they should — nothing more.

Complete Audit Trail

Every decision recorded with mover, seconder, vote count, and outcome. Full meeting history preserved and searchable. Governance records that hold up under scrutiny.

AI Transparency

StrataSphere only answers from your data. Every response is grounded in your actual governance records — never invented, never extrapolated beyond what's on file.

SPA Compliance

Meeting minutes formatted to BC Strata Property Act requirements. Motion language, voting thresholds, and procedural requirements — enforced by the platform, not left to chance.
